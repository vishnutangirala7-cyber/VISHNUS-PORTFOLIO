About the company

Where research becomes flight hardware.

TECHNICAL 7 is an aerospace company dedicated to research and implementation across propulsion, aircraft and space systems. We study the physics, design the systems, and work to turn those designs into working technology.

Our work spans six divisions, each with its own set of focus areas, connected by one idea: propulsion and flight are one engineering discipline, and the best systems are designed together.

01

Research

Studying propulsion physics, aerodynamics, materials and flight dynamics.

02

Design

Modelling and simulation before anything is built, to find problems early.

03

Implementation

Prototyping, testing and refining designs into dependable systems.
